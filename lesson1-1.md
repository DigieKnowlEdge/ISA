# Intro & Onboarding

---

## Lesson Objectives

* Know the names of the trainer and the other participants
* Know where to place open questions and how they will be addressed
* Describe the training requirements and setting expectations
* Describe the course objectives and high level lesson plan
* Know where to get the necessary training materials

---

## Ice Breaker

* Introduce yourself ~ 2 Minutes
* Name, Job Title or Role
* Your knowledge and experience, and Certification in Cyber Security
* E.g. System Security, Risk Management, Secure Software Development Life-Cycle (SDLC), Threat Modeling

---

## Course Objectives

* Describe Information Security Architect's (ISA) role and responsibilities
* Perform Threat Modeling and document the results
* Perform a cloud threat assessment

---

## Course Overview

* Module 1: Foundation
* Module 2: Threat Modeling
* Module 3: Cloud Threat Assessment
* Module 4: ISA Processes
